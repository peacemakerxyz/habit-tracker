# Habit tracker: scope

Goal: a monthly grid where I tick off habits each day and see my totals.

## Must-haves
1. Add a habit (a new row)
2. Click any day to tick or untick it
3. See the monthly total for each habit

## Not now
1. Switching to other months (current month only at first)
2. Streaks, charts, and statistics
3. Accounts or login
